CourseWorthScaled score

Enter your scaled course score for each course you successfully completed (Satisfactory Achievement or higher) — this is the score TASC calculates for you after course scaling, not a raw mark (TASC doesn't publish a raw-to-scaled table; see below). Pick “15 pts” for a normal Level 3/4 course and “8 pts” for a HAP unit or a semester-length UCP unit. Only enter courses from two of your senior-secondary years (Year 11, 12 or 13).
